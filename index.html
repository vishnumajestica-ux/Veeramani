How to play

Jump over the tanks. Every tank you pass adds to your score. A collision costs one life; the game ends when all three lives are gone.

⚡

Controls

SPACE ↑ W or tap the game area to jump. Press P to pause and R to restart.

Made for deployment

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, framework, API key or external image download is required.
